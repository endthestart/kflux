**Decided (2026-10-19): keep "kflux".**

Assessment, which is not legal advice:

- The nearest is **KFluxMod**: UK only, classes 9 and 42, registered this year by Frontier Physics Ltd. The product it covers is unknown.
- kflux would be free, open-source software with no commercial UK trade, so the risk looks low. It's worth knowing about if kflux is ever sold or branded commercially in the UK.
- TMview isn't an official register.
