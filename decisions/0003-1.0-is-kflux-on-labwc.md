# 0003: kflux 1.0 is kflux on labwc; tabs come to labwc

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: accepted.

## Decision

After comparing the three sessions side by side, the maintainer chose this:

- **kflux on labwc is the 1.0 default.** It's the closest to Fluxbox with KDE
  behind it, and it's usable as a daily desktop today.
- **Missing Fluxbox features come to labwc**, starting with tabs. We do the
  work ourselves, either by extending labwc or by contributing upstream.
- **Plasma + Flux Tabs is deprecated.** It isn't what kflux is going for.
  [0002](0002-kwin-tabs-as-a-script.md) stays as a record of the spike.
- **fluxland is parked.** It's a long-term option, worth taking on only as a
  full project. The `fluxland (kflux)` session stays in the dev image as an
  experiment, but it isn't part of the 1.0 release.

## Tabs on labwc: the situation upstream

- labwc's maintainers called Fluxbox-style tabbed windows "out-of-scope" in
  June 2024 ([labwc#1932](https://github.com/labwc/labwc/issues/1932)). They
  also said a client can't do it without "really hacky approaches", so it has
  to live in the compositor.
- In October 2025 a maintainer said the project had outgrown its original
  Openbox-based scope list, and that scope is now "down to maintainers to take
  a view". The door isn't closed, but a feature request alone won't land it.

The plan that fits:

1. Prototype tabs as a patch on labwc. labwc is a small C codebase with an
   Openbox lineage, and Fluxbox's semantics are the model: drag a titlebar
   onto another to group, a tab strip in or above the titlebar, NextTab and
   PrevTab, DetachClient, and the `apps` `[Group]` rule.
2. Ship it in kflux as a `labwc-kflux` package. It replaces labwc and tracks
   upstream releases.
3. Once it's been used daily, open an upstream discussion with the working
   patch and screenshots. If maintainers want it, upstream it; if not, keep
   carrying it, as kflux does today with the fluxland port.

Other Fluxbox gaps to queue behind tabs, from 0001's list:

- keymodes and chained keys
- toolbar auto-hide
- an iconbar that shows only the current workspace (needs workspace info in
  the foreign-toplevel protocol, or a labwc extension)
- fuller style textures: bevels and diagonal gradients
